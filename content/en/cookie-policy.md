## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Some are essential for security or website functionality; others (not used here) track behavior for analytics or advertising.

## Cookies Used on This Site

opa.so uses **two cookies**: one for security and one to remember your language. Neither is used for tracking.

### 1. Strictly Necessary Security Cookie (Cloudflare Turnstile)

- Purpose: Verify legitimate visitors and block bots on the contact form.
- Set by: Cloudflare, only when the contact form's Turnstile check loads.
- Example cookie: `cf_clearance`
- Personal data processed: minimal technical data required for verification.
- Necessity: Required for site security and functionality.
- Legal basis: **Legitimate Interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### 2. Functional Cookie (Locale Preference)

- **Name**: `preferred-locale`
- **Purpose**: Remembers your language preference (English or Spanish) so the correct locale is served on your next visit without requiring a redirect.
- **Set by**: The site itself when you visit any page.
- **Expiry**: 1 year (`max-age=31536000`).
- **Personal data processed**: None — stores only `"en"` or `"es"`.
- **Legal basis**: **Legitimate Interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — required for consistent language routing.

### No Analytics, Marketing, or Tracking Cookies

We do not use Google Analytics, marketing trackers, or any third-party advertising cookies. Our page-view and performance measurement (Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights) does not use cookies.

## Local Storage and Session Storage

The site also saves a few preferences in your browser's storage. These values stay on your device and are not sent to our servers:

- `theme` (local storage): Remembers light, dark, or system theme. Kept until you clear site data.
- `contrast` (local storage): Remembers whether High Contrast mode is on. Kept until you clear site data.
- `preferred-locale` (local storage): Copy of your language preference. Kept until you clear site data.
- `sentryReplaySession` (session storage): Groups session-replay data for a single visit, only when a replay is recorded (see the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy-policy)). Kept until you close the tab.

## Cookie Management

The Turnstile cookie is essential for bot protection and cannot be disabled without breaking the contact form.

You can delete the language cookie and the storage entries above at any time from your browser's site-data settings. The site will then fall back to your browser's language and default display settings.
