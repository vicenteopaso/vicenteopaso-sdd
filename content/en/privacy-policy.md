## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how opa.so ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal data when you visit https://opa.so/. We comply with the EU GDPR, the Spanish AEPD (“Agencia Española de Protección de Datos”), and applicable data-protection regulations.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Data You Provide Voluntarily

When you submit the Contact form, we collect:

- Your email address
- Your name and a subject, if you fill in those optional fields
- Your message content
- Any additional information you voluntarily include

The form is processed by Formspree, our third-party data processor.

To limit abuse, the server keeps a short-lived, in-memory count of contact submissions per IP address. It resets after one minute and is never written to storage.

### 2. Security & Anti-abuse Data (Cloudflare Turnstile)

To protect the contact form from bots and malicious traffic, Cloudflare Turnstile runs on the form and processes:

- IP address
- Browser characteristics
- Interaction data required to verify that you are human

Cloudflare acts as an independent controller for this processing.

### 3. Hosting, Logs & Traffic Data (Vercel)

Because the site is deployed on Vercel, standard hosting logs are automatically collected. These may include:

- IP address
- Timestamp of the request
- URL paths visited
- Device, operating system, and browser type
- Referrer information
- Basic performance and routing metrics (Edge Network telemetry)

Vercel uses this data for:

- Security
- Abuse prevention
- Performance monitoring
- Error diagnostics

Vercel acts as an independent controller.  
Learn more: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

### 4. Aggregate Analytics (Vercel Web Analytics & Speed Insights)

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to count page views and measure page performance (Core Web Vitals). They:

- Do not use cookies
- Do not build profiles or follow you across other websites
- Report aggregated figures, such as pages visited, referrer, country, device type, and browser

Vercel processes this data on our behalf.  
Learn more: https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy

### 5. Error Monitoring (Sentry)

To diagnose bugs and improve reliability, Sentry may process:

- Stack traces and error messages (no personally identifiable data unless included in a form submission)
- Browser type, OS, and URL of the page where the error occurred
- Server-side warnings and errors logged by the site
- Session replay data: recorded for about 10% of visits and for visits where an error occurs, loaded only after you first interact with the page, with all page text and form inputs masked

Sentry acts as a data processor under our instructions.  
https://sentry.io/privacy/

### 6. Found Luggage Page Notifications

The unlisted Found Luggage page (`/found-luggage`) exists so that whoever finds a lost bag can reach the owner. When that page is opened, the site sends a one-time notification to the site owner through Telegram and Formspree containing:

- The time of the visit and the page language
- Approximate location (city and country), derived from your IP address by Vercel
- The referring page, if your browser sends one
- Your browser's user-agent string

Your IP address itself is not included in the notification. No other page sends this notification.

### 7. No Tracking or Advertising Cookies

We do **not** use analytics tools that track identifiable behavior, nor do we use advertising cookies.

## How We Use Your Information

- **Contact form data** is used only to respond to your inquiries.
- **Cloudflare and Vercel data** is used for security, abuse prevention, and website performance.
- **Sentry data** is used only to find and fix errors.
- **Found Luggage notifications** are used only to learn that someone may have found a lost bag.
- We do not sell or share your data for marketing or advertising purposes.

## Lawful Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We rely on:

- **Legitimate Interest:** Responding to inquiries, ensuring website security and stability, measuring aggregate site usage and performance, and recovering lost luggage.
- **Consent:** Provided explicitly when you submit the contact form. You may withdraw your consent at any time.

## Third-Party Data Processors

- **Sentry** — processes error reports, stack traces, server logs, and session replay data for debugging and monitoring.  
  Error data is collected when errors occur. Session replay records about 10% of visits, plus visits where an error occurs.  
  https://sentry.io/privacy/
- **Formspree** — processes contact form submissions and Found Luggage notifications.  
  https://formspree.io/security/
- **Telegram** — delivers Found Luggage notifications to the site owner.  
  https://telegram.org/privacy
- **Cloudflare** — processes security and anti-abuse data as an independent controller.  
  https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
- **Vercel** — processes hosting logs as an independent controller, and aggregate analytics and performance data on our behalf.  
  https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Data Transfers

Formspree, Vercel, Sentry, Cloudflare, and Telegram may process data in the United States or other jurisdictions outside the European Economic Area.  
Transfers are protected using Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and other GDPR-compliant safeguards.

## Data Retention

- Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to respond to you.
- Hosting logs and analytics (Vercel), anti-abuse data (Cloudflare), and error reports (Sentry) follow each provider’s retention policies.
- Found Luggage notifications are kept only until the luggage matter is resolved.
- We do not maintain local or persistent databases of visitor information.

## Your Rights

You may request to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Object to processing
- Withdraw consent
- Request portability of data you provided

To exercise your rights, email: **contact@opa.so**

You may also file a complaint with the AEPD:
